Sartorial Excellence

The Architecture of Fine Bespoke Suiting

Stationed in Soho, Manhattan, FineAttireLab was established to champion full floating horsehair canvas tailoring, Super 150s worsted wool fabrics, and hand-stitched Milanese lapel buttonholes through empirical sartorial research.

Atelier Mandate

Bespoke Hand Canvas Over Mass-Produced Fused Garments

In a menswear market flooded with chemically fused adhesive interlinings that bubble over time, FineAttireLab advocates for loose floating horsehair-silk canvas chest pieces, natural shoulder padding, and hand-padded collar rolls.

Our team of master cutters, textile inspectors, and sartorial historians evaluate suiting through practical laboratory testing: measuring wool fiber micron fineness, thread count resilience, lapel roll drape, and horn button cross-stitch security.

"Bespoke tailoring is living geometry—sculpting fine Australian wool over a floating canvas core that adapts to the wearer's every movement."

Tailoring Principles

Our Four Sartorial Standards

1. Full Canvas Mandate

Rejecting all fused glue interlinings in favor of 100% natural floating horsehair chest canvas.

2. Micron Wool Integrity

Selecting Super 130s to Super 180s worsted cloths woven by historic Biella and Yorkshire mills.

3. Artisan Hand Finishing

Demanding hand-sewn Milanese lapel keyholes, hand-padded collars, and real buffalo horn buttons.

4. Objective Research

100% independent suiting evaluations backed by crease-recovery testing and fabric audits.
